Before Your Appointment

A few simple things can help make your dog's grooming experience as comfortable as possible.

Keep Them Comfortable

A light meal around 2–3 hours before the appointment can help keep your dog comfortable during grooming.

Fresh Water

Make sure your dog has access to fresh water before their appointment.

Tell Us About Your Dog

Let Lindsay know about your dog's temperament, coat condition or any specific grooming requirements when booking.

Relax

Our gentle, one-to-one approach is designed to make grooming as calm and positive as possible.
