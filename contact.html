BEFORE YOU BOOK

A little information helps us give your dog the right care.

When you contact us, it helps to tell us a little about your dog, including their breed, size, coat condition and temperament.

If your dog is nervous, has particular grooming requirements or has any sensitivities we should know about, please mention this when you get in touch.

This allows us to understand your dog's needs before the appointment and make the grooming experience as comfortable as possible.
