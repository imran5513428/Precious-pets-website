PROFESSIONAL PAW CARE

Keeping your dog's nails neat, comfortable and well maintained.

Regular nail care is an important part of your dog's grooming routine. Nails that become too long can affect comfort and movement, which is why we provide careful professional trimming tailored to your dog's needs.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Simple, careful nail care

Our Nail Clipping service focuses on safe, controlled trimming while keeping your dog's comfort at the centre of the appointment.

01

Gentle Paw Handling

We carefully handle your dog's paws and allow them to settle before trimming begins.

02

Nail Assessment

Each nail is assessed before trimming so we can work carefully and maintain an appropriate length.

03

Professional Nail Trim

Nails are carefully trimmed using professional grooming equipment and a controlled technique.

04

All Four Paws

We work through all four paws, checking each nail individually for a neat and comfortable finish.

05

Comfort-Focused Handling

We use calm, patient handling throughout the appointment, especially for dogs that are nervous about having their paws touched.

06

Final Paw Check

Each paw is checked after trimming to ensure the nails are neat and appropriately maintained.

07

Professional Finish

Your dog's nails are left neat and ready for comfortable everyday movement.

08

Calm Experience

We aim to make every appointment as calm and positive as possible, helping dogs become more comfortable with regular nail care.

IS THIS SERVICE RIGHT FOR MY DOG?

Nail Clipping could be a great fit if your dog...

  • Needs their nails trimmed as part of their regular grooming routine.
  • Has nails that have become longer than comfortable.
  • Needs professional help with regular paw and nail maintenance.
  • Is uncomfortable having their paws handled at home.
✦

Gentle handling matters

Some dogs can feel nervous when their paws are handled. We take a calm and patient approach to help make nail care as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

THE RESULT

Neat nails and comfortable paws.

Your dog will leave with neatly trimmed nails and paws that are better maintained for everyday comfort and movement. Regular professional nail care can also make future appointments easier and more familiar for your dog.
