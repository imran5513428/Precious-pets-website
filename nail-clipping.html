PROFESSIONAL PAW CARE

Keeping your dog's nails neat, comfortable and well maintained.

Regular nail care is an important part of your dog's grooming routine. Nails that become too long can affect comfort and movement, which is why we provide careful professional trimming tailored to your dog's needs.

Whether your dog needs regular nail maintenance or simply finds nail trimming difficult at home, our aim is to make the experience calm, careful and as comfortable as possible.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Simple, careful nail care

Our Nail Clipping service focuses on careful trimming while keeping your dog's comfort at the centre of the appointment.

01

Gentle Paw Handling

We carefully handle your dog's paws and allow them to settle before trimming begins.

02

Nail Assessment

Each nail is checked before trimming so we can work carefully and maintain an appropriate length.

03

Professional Nail Trim

Nails are carefully trimmed using professional grooming equipment and a controlled technique.

04

All Four Paws

We work through the paws, checking each nail individually for a neat and comfortable finish.

05

Comfort-Focused Handling

Calm, patient handling is used throughout the appointment, especially for dogs that are nervous about having their paws touched.

06

Final Paw Check

Each paw is checked after trimming to ensure the nails are neat and appropriately maintained.

IS THIS SERVICE RIGHT FOR MY DOG?

Nail Clipping could be a great fit if your dog...

  • Needs their nails trimmed as part of their regular grooming routine.
  • Has nails that have become longer than comfortable.
  • Needs professional help with regular paw and nail maintenance.
  • Is uncomfortable having their paws handled at home.
✦

Gentle handling matters

Some dogs can feel nervous when their paws are handled. We take a calm and patient approach to help make nail care as comfortable as possible.

THE RESULT

Neat nails and comfortable paws.

Your dog will leave with neatly trimmed nails and paws that are better maintained for everyday comfort and movement. Regular professional nail care can also help make future appointments more familiar for your dog.
