A POSITIVE FIRST GROOMING EXPERIENCE

Helping your puppy feel safe, relaxed and confident.

A puppy's first grooming experiences can shape how they feel about grooming for years to come. That's why we focus on creating a calm, positive experience while gently introducing your puppy to the sights, sounds and sensations of professional grooming.

Every puppy is different. Some settle quickly while others need more time to become comfortable with new experiences. We work at a pace your puppy can manage rather than rushing through the appointment.

The aim is not simply to make your puppy look neat. It's to help them start building a positive relationship with grooming from the very beginning.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Gentle care from start to finish

Each puppy grooming appointment is adapted to your puppy's age, temperament, coat and comfort level.

01

Gentle Introduction

We take time to help your puppy become comfortable with the grooming environment and gentle handling.

02

Coat Brushing

Gentle brushing introduces your puppy to regular coat care while helping remove loose hair and manageable minor tangles.

03

Gentle Bath

A gentle bath using suitable professional shampoo helps leave your puppy's coat clean and fresh.

04

Gentle Blow-Dry

Careful drying helps your puppy gradually become familiar with the sound and sensation of professional drying.

05

Face & Paw Tidy

Gentle trimming around the face and paws where appropriate keeps your puppy looking neat and comfortable.

06

Nail Trim

Gentle nail trimming helps your puppy become familiar with having their paws handled as part of regular grooming.

07

Ear Care

Gentle ear cleaning where appropriate helps your puppy become comfortable with routine ear care.

08

Positive Finishing Touch

We finish with reassurance and patience so your puppy can leave feeling comfortable and positive about the experience.

IS THIS SERVICE RIGHT FOR MY PUPPY?

Puppy Grooming could be a great fit if your puppy...

  • Is having their first professional grooming experience.
  • Needs to become comfortable with regular grooming.
  • Is ready to learn that grooming can be a positive experience.
  • Needs gentle coat, paw, face and hygiene care appropriate for their age.
✦

Patience comes first

Puppies are still learning about the world around them. We never want to rush the experience. Our approach is gentle, patient and focused on helping your puppy build positive associations with grooming.

THE RESULT

A confident puppy with a positive grooming experience.

Our goal isn't simply to make your puppy look fresh and tidy. It's to help them become comfortable with grooming so future visits can become easier, calmer and more enjoyable for both you and your dog.
