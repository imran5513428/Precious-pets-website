A LITTLE MORE THAN A BATH

Clean, tidy, fresh and beautifully finished.

Wash & Tidy is designed for dogs who need a little more attention between full grooming appointments. It's a gentle way to keep your dog clean, comfortable and looking neat without the need for a complete haircut.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Thoughtful care with the finishing touches

Every Wash & Tidy appointment combines gentle cleaning with the small details that help your dog look and feel beautifully cared for.

01

Gentle Brushing

A gentle brush to remove loose hair and prepare the coat.

02

Warm Bath

A warm bath using a premium, coat-appropriate shampoo.

03

Blow-Dry & Brush

A thorough blow-dry and final brush for a soft, fluffy finish.

04

Ear Cleaning

Gentle ear cleaning to leave your dog feeling fresh and comfortable.

05

Nail Trim

Nail trimming to support comfort and healthy paws.

06

Finishing Touches

A finishing spray plus a stylish bandana or bow to complete the look.

IDEAL FOR

Wash & Tidy could be a great fit if your dog...

  • Needs regular coat maintenance between full grooms.
  • Needs more than a simple bath and brush.
  • Would benefit from a neat, refreshed appearance.
  • You want your dog looking clean, tidy and photo-ready.
