A LITTLE MORE THAN A WASH

Clean, tidy and comfortable without a full groom.

Wash & Tidy is a good option when your dog needs more than a simple bath but doesn't need a complete haircut. It combines a thorough wash and dry with extra attention to the areas that benefit from a little tidying.

It's particularly useful between full grooms, for dogs whose coats need regular attention, or when you'd like your dog to look neat and feel fresh without changing the overall length or style of their coat.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Thorough care with a tidy finish

Each Wash & Tidy appointment is carried out with your dog's coat, condition and comfort in mind.

01

Coat Preparation

The coat is gently brushed and prepared before bathing, helping remove loose hair and any manageable surface tangles.

02

Thorough Wash

Your dog receives a warm, thorough wash using products selected with their coat and skin needs in mind.

03

Careful Rinse

The coat is rinsed thoroughly to remove shampoo and leave it feeling clean and fresh.

04

Gentle Drying

Your dog is carefully dried with attention to their comfort throughout the drying process.

05

Coat Tidy

Extra attention is given to suitable areas of the coat that need a little tidying, while keeping the overall look natural.

06

Final Finish

A final brush and gentle finishing touches help leave your dog looking neat, clean and comfortable.

IS THIS SERVICE RIGHT FOR MY DOG?

Wash & Tidy could be a great fit if your dog...

  • Needs a good wash but doesn't need a full haircut.
  • Benefits from regular coat tidying between full grooms.
  • Has a coat that needs a little extra attention to stay neat.
  • Needs to stay clean, comfortable and presentable between complete grooming appointments.
