MY APPROACH

Every dog gets treated as an individual.

Dogs don't all experience grooming in the same way. Some are relaxed and confident, while others may need a little more time to settle into the experience. Coat type, age, previous grooming experiences and personality can all make a difference.

That's why I don't believe in rushing through an appointment. I take time to understand each dog, work at a pace they can manage and adjust my approach when they need something different.

The goal is simple: a well-groomed dog who has been handled with patience and respect throughout the process.

WHY MOBILE GROOMING?

A calmer experience, right outside your home.

Precious Pets is a mobile grooming service because I believe the environment can make a real difference to how a dog experiences grooming.

Instead of travelling to a busy salon and waiting around with other dogs, your dog can be groomed close to home in a familiar environment. The one-to-one setting also allows me to focus my attention on your dog throughout their appointment.

For some dogs that means a much more relaxed experience. For others, it's simply a more convenient way for their owners to fit professional grooming into their routine.

✦

One dog. One appointment. One-to-one attention.

The aim is to make grooming feel less like an interruption to your dog's day and more like a calm, familiar part of their routine.
