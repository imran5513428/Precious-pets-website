A COMPLETE GROOMING EXPERIENCE

Professional care tailored to your dog's coat and lifestyle.

Before we begin, we'll take a few minutes to understand your dog's coat, lifestyle and the look you'd like to achieve. We'll then recommend the most suitable grooming style before starting the haircut.

The aim is not simply to give your dog a haircut. It's to provide a complete grooming experience that leaves them clean, comfortable and looking their very best.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Complete care from start to finish

Every Full Groom appointment is carefully carried out with your dog's comfort, coat condition and grooming needs in mind.

01

Gentle Coat Brushing

Gentle brushing to remove loose hair and manageable tangles before grooming begins.

02

Professional Bath

A professional bath using shampoo selected with your dog's coat and skin needs in mind.

03

Blow-Dry & Brush

Thorough drying followed by careful brushing to prepare the coat for its final groom.

04

Professional Haircut

A haircut tailored to your dog's coat, needs and the look you'd like to achieve.

05

Sanitary & Paw Trim

Careful trimming around suitable sanitary areas and paw pads to support hygiene, comfort and grip.

06

Ear Cleaning & Nail Trim

Gentle ear cleaning and nail trimming to help keep your dog comfortable and well cared for.

07

Fresh Finishing Spray

A light finishing spray to leave the coat smelling fresh and looking beautifully finished.

08

Complimentary Finishing Touch

A complimentary bandana or bow to complete your dog's freshly groomed look.

IS THIS SERVICE RIGHT FOR MY DOG?

Full Groom could be a great fit if your dog...

  • Needs a complete professional grooming session.
  • Would benefit from a professional haircut and coat tidy.
  • Needs regular grooming to keep their coat clean and manageable.
  • You want a complete clean, tidy and beautifully finished look.
