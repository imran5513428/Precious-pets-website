A SIMPLE, FRESH START

Clean, comfortable and ready to head home.

Wash & Go is for those times when your dog needs a proper clean without the need for a haircut. It is a straightforward option for keeping their coat fresh, clean and comfortable as part of their regular grooming routine.

It can be a good choice between full grooming appointments, after your dog has simply got a little grubby, or whenever they need a freshen-up without having their coat fully restyled.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Gentle care from start to finish

Your dog's Wash & Go appointment includes the essential steps needed to leave their coat clean, fresh and comfortable.

01

Gentle Brushing

A gentle brush to remove loose hair and prepare your dog's coat before bathing.

02

Warm Bath

A warm bath using a shampoo selected with your dog's coat and skin needs in mind.

03

Thorough Rinse

A thorough rinse helps remove shampoo completely and leaves the coat feeling clean.

04

Careful Drying

Careful drying helps leave your dog clean, comfortable and ready to head home.

05

Final Brush

A final brush helps give the coat a neat and comfortable finish.

06

Finishing Touches

Gentle finishing touches are provided where appropriate for your dog's needs.

IS THIS SERVICE RIGHT FOR MY DOG?

Wash & Go could be a great fit if your dog...

  • Needs a fresh bath but not a haircut.
  • Has a healthy coat that simply needs cleaning.
  • Is between full grooming appointments.
  • Enjoys regular grooming to stay clean and comfortable.
✦

A fresh coat without the full groom

Wash & Go gives you a simple way to keep your dog's coat clean and fresh without booking a full haircut and styling appointment.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Fresh, clean and ready for home.

Your dog will leave with a clean, freshly washed coat and a neat, comfortable finish, ready to get back to their normal routine.
