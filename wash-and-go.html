A SIMPLE, FRESH START

Clean, comfortable and ready to head home.

Wash & Go is designed for those times when your dog needs a proper clean without the need for a haircut. It's a gentle, practical way to keep your dog's coat fresh and comfortable between full grooming appointments.

Whether your dog needs a regular bath, a freshen-up between full grooms or simply enjoys staying clean and comfortable, this service provides a calm and straightforward grooming experience.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Gentle care from start to finish

Every Wash & Go appointment is carried out with care, using an approach suited to your dog's coat, skin and comfort.

01

Gentle Brushing

A gentle brush to remove loose hair and prepare your dog's coat before bathing.

02

Warm Bath

A warm bath using a shampoo selected with your dog's coat and skin needs in mind.

03

Thorough Rinse

A thorough rinse helps remove shampoo completely and leaves the coat feeling clean.

04

Careful Drying

Careful drying helps leave your dog clean, comfortable and ready to head home.

05

Final Brush

A final brush helps give the coat a neat and comfortable finish.

06

Finishing Touches

Gentle finishing touches are provided where appropriate for your dog's needs.

IS THIS SERVICE RIGHT FOR MY DOG?

Wash & Go could be a great fit if your dog...

  • Needs a fresh bath but not a haircut.
  • Has a healthy coat that simply needs cleaning.
  • Is between full grooming appointments.
  • Enjoys regular grooming to stay clean and comfortable.
