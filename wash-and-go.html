A SIMPLE, FRESH START

Clean, comfortable and ready to head home.

Wash & Go is designed for those times when your dog needs a proper clean without the need for a haircut. It's a gentle, practical way to keep your dog's coat fresh and comfortable between full grooming appointments.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Gentle care from start to finish

Every Wash & Go appointment is carried out with care, using an approach suited to your dog's coat and skin.

01

Gentle Brushing

A gentle brush to remove loose hair before bathing.

02

Warm Bath

A warm bath using a shampoo chosen for your dog's coat and skin type.

03

Thorough Rinse

A thorough rinse to remove shampoo completely.

04

Careful Drying

Careful drying for a clean, comfortable coat.

05

Final Brush

A final brush for a neat finish.

06

Finishing Touches

A few gentle finishing touches where appropriate.

IS THIS SERVICE RIGHT FOR MY DOG?

Wash & Go could be a great fit if your dog...

  • Needs a fresh bath but not a haircut.
  • Has a healthy coat that just needs cleaning.
  • Is between full grooming appointments.
  • Enjoys regular grooming to stay comfortable.
