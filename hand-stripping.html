Hand Stripping

Specialist coat care that preserves your dog's natural texture.

Hand stripping is a specialist grooming technique designed for wire-haired and certain double-coated breeds. By carefully removing mature outer coat by hand, the natural texture, colour and appearance of the coat can be maintained.

SPECIALIST COAT CARE

Maintaining the natural character of a hand-stripped coat.

Hand stripping is different from simply clipping a dog's coat. The technique involves carefully removing mature outer hairs by hand to help maintain the coat's natural texture and appearance. We assess your dog's coat and grooming requirements before beginning the service.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Careful, specialist coat preparation

Each hand stripping appointment is tailored to your dog's coat condition, breed characteristics and grooming requirements.

Coat Preparation

IS THIS SERVICE RIGHT FOR MY DOG?

Hand Stripping could be a great fit if your dog...

  • Has a wire-haired or suitable coat for hand stripping.
  • Needs specialist coat maintenance rather than routine clipping.
  • You want to help preserve the natural texture of their coat.
  • Needs regular professional coat management.
✦

A specialist grooming technique

Hand stripping isn't suitable for every dog. We'll assess your dog's coat and discuss their grooming needs to determine whether this technique is appropriate before we begin.

THE RESULT

A natural-looking, well-maintained coat.

A professionally hand-stripped coat can retain its characteristic texture and natural appearance while giving your dog a neat, well-maintained finish. The result is a groom tailored to the individual needs of your dog's coat.
