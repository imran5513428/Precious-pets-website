SPECIALIST COAT CARE

Maintaining the natural character of a hand-stripped coat.

Hand stripping is different from simply clipping a dog's coat. The technique involves carefully removing mature outer hairs by hand to help maintain the coat's natural texture and appearance.

Before the service begins, we assess your dog's coat condition, texture and grooming requirements to determine whether hand stripping is suitable and how the coat should be approached.

IS THIS SERVICE RIGHT FOR MY DOG?

Hand Stripping could be a great fit if your dog...

  • Has a wire-haired or otherwise suitable coat for hand stripping.
  • Needs specialist coat maintenance rather than routine clipping.
  • You want to help preserve the natural texture of their coat.
  • Needs regular professional coat management.

A specialist grooming technique

Hand stripping isn't suitable for every dog. We'll assess your dog's coat and discuss their grooming needs to determine whether this technique is appropriate before we begin.
