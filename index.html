Appointments typically take 1–3 hours depending on the service, your dog's size, coat condition and temperament. A simple wash and tidy might take 90 minutes, while a full groom with hand stripping could take 3 hours. We'll give you an exact timeframe when you book.

Yes. We're a mobile grooming service. Our purpose-built van comes directly to your home across London. This means your dog stays in familiar surroundings, reducing stress and anxiety.
